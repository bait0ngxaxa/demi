## 1. Purpose

This register is the current product and architecture source of truth for the Business Flow Closure Program. It decomposes the customer whiteboard into individual outcomes so later audits can verify the complete chain:

Customer intent → actor → entry point → operation → inputs → domain transition → canonical persistence → authorization → readback/history → downstream consumer → runtime evidence → gap/decision → dependency/order.

The register audits semantic and lifecycle equivalence. A route, page, model, test label, or historical IMPLEMENTED status by itself does not prove that a customer capability is complete. The rows are an inventory and audit baseline, not authorization to build every capability they describe.

## 9. Known conflicts and decision gates

These are explicit audit topics, not implied implementation decisions:

1. **DOB password:** SUPERSEDED by one-time activation and a Patient-owned password; first-login intent retained.
2. **Rounds 1–6:** whiteboard round choreography must be mapped to normalized Program, Service 1, Goal Plan, Follow-up and Final. A generic Follow-up route is not semantic equivalence.
3. **Health Plan:** PatientGoalPlan is not automatically “แผนสุขภาพ” or “แผนสุขภาพดีของฉัน”.
4. **PAM/PROMs and named tools:** current prototype semantics/scoring are not final customer-approved clinical instruments.
5. **Patient plus service:** separately audit service request, Patient-authored clinical data, operator care record, and OSM assignment. CARE-07 request does not create any of the latter records.
6. **Appointment postponement:** whiteboard contains response/reschedule/postponement; current accepted behavior has acknowledgement, cancellation request, Hospital-managed scheduling and, since Phase B (WB-DF-06), a Patient/OSM reschedule-request flow approved through the existing staff edit. Whether the approved time must match a preferred option remains open as WB-PD-11.
7. **Family scope:** relationship is not authority to read care plans, medication, or all appointments. Current accepted delegated v1 is a narrow appointment grant and has a real-data governance gate.
8. **Hospital staff vs Hospital Admin governance:** compare user/OSM/staff add, edit, delete, and download authority with direct MEMBER/OWNER policy operation by operation. Clarify delete vs suspend/history retention and the actual Patient–OSM assignment authority. Do not widen MEMBER by inference.
9. **Responsibility Area:** do not equate with parentHospitalId, Hospital hierarchy, OSM–Hospital relationship, PatientOsmAssignment, geography, or authorization scope.
10. **Notifications:** record event source, schedule/time-slot domain, recipient, preference, consent, transport, delivery/outcome, and real provider/device UAT separately. Whiteboard evidence available here does not conclusively bind named time slots to medication; automated source logic is not proof of delivery.
11. **Consent and privacy:** W2 explicitly names Terms/use, Privacy Policy, personal health-data disclosure consent, and marketing-purpose disclosure consent as distinct customer concepts. Legal/controller owner, purpose, version, affirmative evidence, actor/time/scope, withdrawal, processing effect, retention and audit remain requirement-gated. None is an ordinary setting; Terms is not Privacy Policy and neither substitutes for a consent purpose.
12. **Reporting:** available report/export does not validate upstream source meaning, clinical definition, data capture or authorization for another actor.
13. **Patient profile/measurements:** BMI, CVD risk, occupation, diagnosis, family history, blood pressure/glucose units, “current” value and effective date are not inferred from a similarly named column or classification.
14. **Family QR:** QR is a transport for an identity-bound invitation; scanning alone is not relationship acceptance or an access grant.
