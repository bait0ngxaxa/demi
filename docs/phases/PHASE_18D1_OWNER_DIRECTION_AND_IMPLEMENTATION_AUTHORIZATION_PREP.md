### B1 — Eligible population (Customer/Product, Hospital operations/data owner; Security; Architecture/DB)

Proposed precise predicate for review: authenticated/current actor is eligible per B3; target H is ACTIVE and exact; P.status IN {ACTIVE, COMPLETED}; P.patientHospitalRelationshipId=R.id; R.hospitalId=H.id; R.patientProfile.person.user exists with PATIENT role. **No Patient User status filter**, activation requirement, R “active” flag, date window, classification filter, OSM assignment filter, Person-wide join, or implicit latest-P selection. Require approval of this predicate, not merely “active patients”.

Existing read/provisioning precedent includes provisioned Patient accounts and PATIENT-role eligibility; login activation governs account access, not proof of active treatment. Operations must explicitly decide inclusion for PROVISIONED/INVITED/ACTIVE/SUSPENDED Patient Users, removed PATIENT role, and any valid profile/relationship lacking eligible User/role. Proposed inclusion of all account statuses having PATIENT role is **not accepted**; suspension may require a distinct privacy/operational exclusion. If eligibility requires treatment status/history not stored, stop and narrow or seek separate source design; do not invent fields.

No-P R produces no row; one R with completed A then active B produces two; same Person in H A/B never joins/dedups across H. No clinical record source needed. Evidence to close: approved predicate + status/provisioning table, grain/no-P examples, scope/version/effective conditions, customer/operations acceptance and Security/Architecture review. Risk if skipped: hidden omissions, false active-treatment claim, cross-H disclosure.
